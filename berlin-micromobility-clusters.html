#NearVehiclesNextbikeDott
Vehicle data: live GBFS free_bike_status feeds published directly by Nextbike Berlin and Dott Berlin, both listed in the MobilityData GBFS registry as open, public, no-auth feeds — fetched directly from your browser, nothing is proxied through a server. Clustering is a simple client-side grid binning of live positions, not an official concentration or demand metric. Coverage depends on which operators keep their feeds public at any given time; other Berlin operators (TIER, Voi, Bolt, Lime…) are not included here because they do not currently publish an open GBFS feed for Berlin. Basemap: CARTO Dark Matter (free, no API key).
