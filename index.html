How I work

A planner's questions, answered with working software.

  1. 01 — Planning

    Start from the street

    Trained as an urban planner, I start with how people actually move — on foot, by bike, on transit — and what a planner or commuter needs to decide.

  2. 02 — Analysis

    Get the geodata right

    QGIS and ArcGIS, OpenStreetMap, GTFS and open government APIs: I find the data, clean it and model it — isochrones, line-of-sight, shade scores.

  3. 03 — Delivery

    Ship it as a tool

    Using AI-assisted development, I turn analysis into fast, accessible web apps that anyone can open — not a static PDF that ends up in a drawer.

About

Based in Berlin. Always on a bike or a train.

I'm an urban planner and GIS analyst and a Technion graduate, fascinated by how cities move. I cycle whenever I can and take public transport everywhere else — which is where most of these projects started.

I'm not a traditional software developer. I lean on AI-assisted development to turn spatial analysis into data tools that help planners and commuters understand urban mobility — and I care that those tools are accessible and respect people's privacy.
