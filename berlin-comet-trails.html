Berlin Alive: Public Transit in Motion

This tool records live vehicle positions across Berlin straight in your browser via VBB (v6.vbb.transport.rest/radar), draws them as fading comet trails over a dark basemap, and lets you export it all as smooth video (WebM) to download.

Drag and zoom the map to choose the area and scale for the animation, then confirm.
