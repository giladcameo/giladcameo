Privacy

This map loads tiles from Esri (server.arcgisonline.com). Only when you search does it send the addresses you type to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), your start/end points to the FOSSGIS routing server (routing.openstreetmap.de) and the route area to the Overpass API (overpass-api.de); a point you pick on the map is sent to Nominatim to look up its address. Your IP address is transmitted to these services. Your location is never requested. More: Datenschutz.

Interactive map. After a search it shows the walking routes between A and B, coloured green where shaded and amber where exposed to sun, plus tree canopies and building shadows. The same results (shade percentage, distance, walk time and alternatives) are listed in the route results panel. When a pick button is active, you can also pan the map with the arrow keys and press Enter to set the point at the map centre.
